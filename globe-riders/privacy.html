Globe Riders Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Globe Riders ("the game", "we", "us") is a motorbike stunt game made by DenbroTech. This policy explains what information the game handles and why.

1. No account, nothing sent to us

Globe Riders has no sign-up or account. Your game progress (scores, leaderboard entries, unlocked bikes, paints and outfits, and your settings) is stored only on your own device. We do not run servers that collect it, and we cannot see it.

2. Google Play Games leaderboards

Globe Riders uses Google Play Games Services for online leaderboards. If you use Google Play Games on your device, the game signs you in through it and sends your best scores (show points, best combo, perfects and free ride time) to Google's leaderboards, where other players can see them next to your Play Games profile name and picture. Google also receives your Play Games player ID and basic technical information needed to run the service.

This information is handled by Google, not by us, under the Google Privacy Policy. You can choose not to use Google Play Games, change who can see your profile, or delete your Play Games data at any time in the Google Play Games app (Profile → Settings).

3. Advertising

The current version of Globe Riders does not show real ads and does not include any advertising service. If ads are added in a future version, this policy will be updated before that version is released to explain what the ad provider collects.

4. What we don't collect

  • We don't ask you to create an account or collect your name, email address, phone number or contacts.
  • We don't use your location, camera, microphone or photos.
  • We don't use analytics or tracking tools.
  • We don't sell any information, and apart from the Google Play Games leaderboards described above we don't share any.

5. Deleting your information

Because your progress lives only on your device, you can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data (Settings → Apps → Globe Riders → Storage → Clear data) or by uninstalling the app. Leaderboard scores held by Google Play Games can be deleted in the Google Play Games app.

6. Children

We do not collect personal information from anyone, including children. Google Play Games is only used if it is available and set up on the device; it follows Google's own rules for children's accounts.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.

8. Contact us
