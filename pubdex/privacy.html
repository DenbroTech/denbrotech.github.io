PubDex Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

PubDex ("we", "us") is a pub-logging game made by DenbroTech. This policy explains what information PubDex handles, where it is kept and how you can delete it.

1. Using PubDex without an account

You can use PubDex without signing in. Your pubs, visits, ratings, beers, badges, photos and settings are then stored only on your own device.

2. If you create an account

Signing in lets you save your game to your account, add friends and share pubs. When you have an account we store:

  • Account details: your email address and a securely hashed password (or a sign-in link sent to your email).
  • Your profile: your player name, avatar, player card style, hometown and whether your profile is public or private.
  • Your game: the pubs you've logged (name, address and map position), your visits, ratings, beers, badges, levels and lists such as your wishlist.
  • Things you share: pubs you add to the shared PubDex pub map, name-change suggestions and votes, comments, replies and likes, and reports.
  • Friends: friend requests and who your friends are.
  • Notifications: if you allow them, a push notification token for your device, so we can tell you about friend requests, comments and likes.

Photos you add to a pub stay on your device and are not uploaded.

3. Who can see what

  • Your friends can see your player card and your pubs.
  • If your profile is public, other players can see your player card.
  • Pubs added to the shared PubDex map, comments and name-change suggestions can be seen by other players.
  • Your email address is never shown to other players.

4. Your location

If you allow it, PubDex uses your location while the app is open to centre the map and find pubs near you. Your live location is not stored on our servers and is not shared with other players. Only the positions of the pubs you log are saved.

To show maps, search for places and plan routes, the app sends map positions and search words to these map services: MapTiler (map pictures), OpenStreetMap services Nominatim and Overpass (place search), and OSRM (routes). They receive your device's IP address as part of each request.

5. Where your information is kept

Account information is stored with Supabase, our database provider, and our website runs on Cloudflare. They store and process it on our behalf and may hold it in data centres in other countries, with appropriate safeguards in place.

6. What we don't do

  • We don't sell your information.
  • PubDex does not show advertising and does not use your information for advertising.
  • We don't use third-party analytics or tracking tools.

7. Deleting your information

  • In the app: open the menu, then Account, then Delete account. This deletes your account and the information stored with it.
  • By email: send a request from your account's email address to denbrotech@proton.me and we will delete your account within 30 days.
  • On your device: clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

Pubs you added to the shared pub map may stay on the map after your account is deleted, without your name on them, so other players can keep using them.

8. Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your information, ask us to correct it or delete it, or object to how we use it. Email denbrotech@proton.me. You can also complain to the privacy regulator where you live.

9. Age

PubDex is about pubs and beer and is meant for adults of legal drinking age. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
