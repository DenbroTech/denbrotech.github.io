Flippy Mat Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Flippy Mat: Beer Mat Flip ("Flippy Mat", "we", "us") is a game made by DenbroTech. This policy explains what information the game handles and why.

1. No account, nothing sent to us

Flippy Mat has no sign-up or account. Your game progress (scores, streaks, the beer mats you've won, and your settings) is stored only on your own device. We do not run servers that collect it, and we cannot see it.

2. Advertising

Flippy Mat shows a banner ad, provided by Google AdMob. The app asks Google for non-personalised ads only, which means ads are not chosen based on your past activity or interests.

To show ads, measure them, limit how often you see the same ad and stop fraud, Google AdMob may collect information from your device, such as:

  • your device's advertising ID (or an app-specific identifier)
  • your IP address, which can give an approximate location (country or city)
  • device and app information, such as the phone model, operating system, language and app version
  • which ads were shown and whether they were tapped

This information goes to Google, not to us. You can read how Google uses it at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

3. Sharing your score

If you choose to share a score card or a replay, the app makes a picture or video on your device and hands it to your phone's share menu. Where it goes from there (for example WhatsApp or Instagram) is up to you and that service's own privacy policy. Nothing is sent to us.

4. What we don't collect

  • We don't collect your name, email address, phone number or contacts.
  • We don't use your precise location, camera, microphone or photos.
  • We don't use analytics or tracking tools of our own.
  • We don't sell any information.

5. Deleting your information

Because your progress lives only on your device, you can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data (Settings → Apps → Flippy Mat → Storage → Clear data) or by uninstalling the app.

6. Age

Flippy Mat has a pub and beer theme and is meant for adults. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
