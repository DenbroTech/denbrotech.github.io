Globe of Death Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Globe of Death ("the game", "we", "us") is a motorbike stunt game made by DenbroTech. This policy explains what information the game handles and why.

1. No account, nothing sent to us

Globe of Death has no sign-up or account. Your game progress (scores, leaderboard entries, unlocked bikes, paints and outfits, and your settings) is stored only on your own device. We do not run servers that collect it, and we cannot see it.

2. Advertising

The current version of Globe of Death does not show real ads and does not include any advertising service. If ads are added in a future version, this policy will be updated before that version is released to explain what the ad provider collects.

3. What we don't collect

  • We don't ask you to create an account or collect your name, email address, phone number or contacts.
  • We don't use your location, camera, microphone or photos.
  • We don't use analytics or tracking tools.
  • We don't sell or share any information.

4. Deleting your information

Because your progress lives only on your device, you can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data (Settings → Apps → Globe of Death → Storage → Clear data) or by uninstalling the app.

5. Children

Globe of Death does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.

7. Contact us

Questions about this policy or about Globe of Death: denbrotech@proton.me
