Flippy Mat

The pub game you already know. Flip a stack of beer mats off the edge of the table and catch them. Then try two.

Bet you can't flip it

  • 1. SlidePush the mats to the edge of the table, just far enough to get your fingers under.
  • 2. FlipFlick them up off the edge and watch them spin.
  • 3. CatchPinch them out of the air before they hit the floor. Too steep and they squirt out of your fingers.
  • 4. StackCaught one? Now try two, then three... Beat your best and climb the leaderboard.

Win 30 beer mats

Every stack you catch wins you a new mat to flip, from pub classics to fire-breathing dragons, plus shiny metal mats and secret bonus mats. Many of them come to life when you play.
