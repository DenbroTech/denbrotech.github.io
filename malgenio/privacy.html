Malgenio Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 September 2026

Malgenio ("Malgenio", "we", "us", or "our") is a mood and lifestyle tracking application designed to help users record and understand patterns in their daily experiences, moods, lifestyle and menstrual cycle.

Your privacy is important to us. Malgenio is designed so that the information you enter into the app is stored locally on your device. Malgenio does not require you to create an account and does not send your personal tracking information to our servers.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Malgenio handles, how it is used, where it is stored, and how you can delete it.

1. Information Malgenio Stores

Malgenio allows you to enter information about your daily experiences and lifestyle. Depending on how you use the app, this may include:

  • Mood information
  • Lifestyle or daily activity information
  • Personal notes or journal-style entries
  • Menstrual cycle information, including information you enter about your periods
  • Information derived from your entries, such as estimated or predicted future menstrual-cycle dates
  • Other information that you voluntarily enter into the app

Some of this information, particularly menstrual-cycle information, may constitute health or sensitive personal information.

Malgenio does not require you to provide your name, email address, telephone number, postal address or other direct identifying information.

2. How Your Information Is Stored

Information entered into Malgenio is stored locally on your device.

Malgenio does not operate a user account system or require you to create an account.

Malgenio does not upload your mood, lifestyle, journal or menstrual-cycle information to a Malgenio server or cloud database.

We do not have access to the information you enter into the app.

If your device provides its own backup functionality, such as Android device backup, information stored by an application may potentially be included in a device backup according to your device's operating-system settings. Such backups are controlled by the device and operating-system provider rather than by Malgenio.

3. How Your Information Is Used

Information you enter into Malgenio is used to provide the features of the app, including:

  • Recording and displaying your mood and lifestyle information
  • Allowing you to review your previous entries
  • Identifying patterns in the information you have recorded
  • Displaying relationships or trends between your recorded information and different stages of your menstrual cycle
  • Estimating or predicting future menstrual-cycle dates based on information you have entered
  • Providing optional reminders to encourage you to record your daily information

Malgenio does not use your information for advertising, marketing, profiling or sale to third parties.

4. Menstrual Cycle Information

Malgenio includes menstrual-cycle tracking and prediction functionality.

You may voluntarily enter information about your menstrual cycle. Malgenio may use this information to estimate future cycle dates and to display patterns in your recorded mood or lifestyle information in relation to different stages of your cycle.

Menstrual-cycle predictions are estimates based on information entered by you and should not be relied upon as an exact prediction of when a period will occur.

Malgenio is intended for personal tracking and informational purposes. It does not provide medical diagnosis, medical treatment, medical advice, contraception advice, fertility treatment, or emergency medical advice.

You should consult an appropriately qualified healthcare professional if you have questions or concerns about your menstrual cycle, reproductive health, mood, or other health-related matters.

5. Notifications

Malgenio may provide optional notifications to remind you to record your daily information.

Notifications are not enabled by default unless you choose to enable them.

If you enable reminders, Malgenio may schedule notifications on your device. These reminders are used solely to provide the reminder functionality you have requested.

You can disable notifications at any time through Malgenio's settings or your device's notification settings.

6. Information We Do Not Collect

Malgenio does not collect or transmit the following for its own purposes:

  • Names
  • Email addresses
  • Telephone numbers
  • Postal addresses
  • User account credentials
  • Contacts
  • Precise location information
  • Photos or videos
  • Microphone recordings
  • Advertising identifiers for advertising purposes
  • Analytics information
  • Behavioural advertising information
  • Payment information
  • Health Connect data
  • Information from other health or fitness services

Malgenio does not contain advertising and does not use your information to deliver personalised advertisements.

7. Third-Party Services

Malgenio does not use third-party analytics services, advertising networks, artificial-intelligence services, or external databases to process the information you enter into the app.

Malgenio does not sell, rent or otherwise provide your personal tracking information to third parties.

The Android operating system and your device manufacturer may provide operating-system-level services, such as device backup, notification delivery and security features. Those services operate according to their own privacy policies and settings.

8. Data Sharing

Because Malgenio is designed to store your tracking information locally on your device, Malgenio does not routinely transmit that information to us or share it with third parties.

We do not sell your personal information.

We do not share your mood, lifestyle, journal or menstrual-cycle information with advertisers, data brokers or other third parties.

9. Data Retention

Your tracking information remains on your device for as long as you keep it in Malgenio.

Malgenio does not maintain a remote copy of your tracking information.

You can remove your stored Malgenio information by using any deletion or reset functionality provided within the app, where available, or by clearing Malgenio's application data or uninstalling Malgenio from your device.

Uninstalling the app removes locally stored application data according to the Android operating system. If your device has independently backed up application data, you may also need to manage or delete that backup through your device or operating-system backup settings.

10. Data Security

Because your tracking information is stored locally on your device rather than on Malgenio's servers, protection of that information also depends on the security of your device.

We recommend using the security features provided by your device, such as a screen lock, PIN, password or biometric authentication.

Malgenio does not intentionally transmit your tracking information to a remote Malgenio server.

11. Children's Privacy

Malgenio is not specifically designed for children.

Malgenio does not knowingly collect personal information from children through accounts or online services because Malgenio does not require accounts and does not operate a remote user database.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us through another means, please contact us using the details below.

12. Your Privacy Choices

You control the information you enter into Malgenio.

You can choose whether to:

  • Enter or remove tracking information
  • Enable or disable daily reminders
  • Continue using or stop using particular tracking features
  • Delete locally stored application data
  • Uninstall the application

Because Malgenio does not maintain an online user account or remote database containing your tracking information, there is no Malgenio account that you need to request to be closed.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to Malgenio, applicable laws, or our privacy practices.

When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this Privacy Policy.

You should review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
